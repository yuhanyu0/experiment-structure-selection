# Claim Boundary

## Supported

- The frozen MESA-2 Final decision is `PASS_MESA2_GEOMETRY_SELECTS_EXPERIMENT_STRUCTURE`.
- Initial coarse evidence is exactly aliased across the declared mechanism candidates to machine precision in the frozen Final audit.
- In `ONE_STEP_SUFFICIENT`, the first selected program is depth 1 with the `final_state` bundle in 8/8 sealed Final seeds.
- In `COMPOSITION_REQUIRED`, the first selected program is depth 3 with the `final_state` bundle in 8/8 sealed Final seeds.
- In `READOUT_REQUIRED`, the first selected program is depth 1 with the `microshape` bundle in 8/8 sealed Final seeds.
- The full grammar beats the declared depth/readout/random/fixed baselines where those restrictions remove the information required by the source family, under the frozen exact sign tests.
- Search and evaluation contexts and intervention-word families are disjoint under the frozen build audit.
- The result supports evidence-conditioned **experiment-structure selection within the declared synthetic families, grammar, costs and held registry**.

## Not supported

- The selected experiment is globally minimal.
- MESA-2 discovers a natural physical or biological mechanism.
- The three synthetic families exhaust the ways experiments can be insufficient.
- The experiment grammar is universal.
- Posterior-predictive covariance geometry is a uniquely correct acquisition rule.
- MESA-2 identifies the surface-minimization mechanism in the exact-surface solver.
- Automatic informative-experiment selection is new.
- Equivalence-class refinement, distinguishing-sequence search, model discrimination, active input design, sensor selection, or predictive-state ideas are new because they appear here.
- The synthetic Final establishes transfer to an external simulator or empirical system.

## Unresolved

- Literature novelty of the combined problem formulation and benchmark protocol.
- External transfer to a simulator and rival-model set not engineered for MESA-2.
- Sensitivity to alternative mechanism priors, cost models, acquisition objectives and larger version spaces.
- Whether experiment-structure selection remains reliable when outcomes require approximate simulation or learned surrogates rather than exact target means under the declared likelihood.
- Global or approximate minimality under a broader experiment grammar.
