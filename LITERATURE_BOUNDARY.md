# Literature Boundary

MESA-2 is not presented as the invention of informative experiment design.

The frozen literature audit treats the following as established neighboring areas rather than novelty claims:

- optimal and Bayesian experimental design for rival-model discrimination;
- adaptive / sequential experimental design and information-gain policies;
- active causal discovery that refines equivalence classes by intervention;
- active automata learning and distinguishing sequences;
- system identification and informative input design;
- sensor selection and active feature acquisition;
- predictive-state representations based on future observable tests.

Accordingly, MESA-2 does **not** claim novelty merely for choosing informative experiments, shrinking a mechanism equivalence class, searching for distinguishing inputs, choosing sensors/readouts, balancing information and cost, or defining state through future tests.

The strongest currently defensible contribution is narrower:

> **a problem formulation, benchmark structure and claim-bounded synthesis protocol for selecting which component of the next experiment must become richer first — intervention depth, observation richness, or an ordinary one-step state measurement — conditioned on the current surviving mechanism version space.**

Formal literature novelty for that combined formulation remains **not established** in the frozen MESA-2 record.
